# Privacy Policy — Stop Ahead (English)

Effective date: 2026-10-18

## Summary

Stop Ahead is a privacy-first commuter reminder app. Your data stays on your device. We do not track you, we do not sell your data, and we do not use third-party analytics or advertising identifiers.

## What the app stores

- The places you choose to save (name, category, coordinates of the place, reminder radius).
- Reminder settings (quiet hours, sound, vibration).
- A local history of reminder events (when an alert was shown, dismissed, or snoozed) — this stores outcomes, never your location path.
- A local technical diagnostic log (system events such as permission changes or geofence errors) used only to help fix problems you report.

## What we collect

Nothing leaves your device automatically. No analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no account required.

## Location

The app uses your location only to detect when you enter the reminder zones you created. Location is used for geofencing only; your position history is never recorded or transmitted.

## Diagnostics sharing (optional)

If you contact support about a problem, you may choose to share a diagnostic report. This report is a technical file containing your app version, OS version, recent system events, and reminder outcomes. It contains no location coordinates and no personal content. It is shared only when you explicitly send it.

## Data retention

Reminder history is kept locally and automatically deleted after 30 days (configurable). You can delete all your data at any time from Settings → Delete all my data.

## Contact

For privacy questions, contact the support email listed in the app.

## Changes

This policy is versioned with the app. Any change will be reflected here with an updated effective date.
