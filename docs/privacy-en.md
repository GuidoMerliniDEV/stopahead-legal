# Privacy Policy — Stop Ahead (English)

Effective date: 2026-11-04

## Summary

Stop Ahead is a privacy-first commuter reminder app. Your data stays on your device. We do not track you, we do not sell your data, and we do not use third-party analytics or advertising identifiers.

## What the app stores

- The places you choose to save (name, category, reminder radius, and the coordinates of the place, taken from your device's position at the moment you add it; if no position is available the place is saved by name only and is not monitored).
- Reminder settings (quiet hours).
- If you choose Tell someone I am arriving, the selected contact name, phone number and optional message are stored on this device so the messaging app can be prepared when an alert arrives. The app never sends the message automatically, and the message never includes your location.
- A local history of reminder events (when an alert was shown, dismissed, or snoozed) — this stores outcomes and the place name you chose, never your location path. History entries are deleted automatically after 30 days or with Delete all my data.
- A local technical diagnostic log (system events such as permission changes, geofence errors, or app crashes) used only to help fix problems. Each time the system reports that you crossed the zone of a place, it also keeps one line with coarse buckets only: how accurate the position fix was, whether the phone was still or moving, whether the fix was inside or outside the zone, and what the app decided. It never contains coordinates or the place name. The log is deleted automatically after 30 days or with Delete all my data.

This data is excluded from device and cloud backups on both Android and iOS: it exists only on this device.

If you want to keep or move your places, Settings can export them (with their address text, alert rules, quiet hours, alarm and heads-up settings, alarm-after-leaving rules and schedules) to a file that you choose and import such a file later. Contact names, phone numbers and messages are never included. The file is created and read on your device; the app never uploads it. Your history and diagnostics are never included.

## What we collect

Nothing leaves your device automatically, with two user-controlled exceptions: the map described below, and opening your selected messaging app from a Tell someone alert. The message is not sent by Stop Ahead; it is sent only if you choose Send in that app. No analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no account required.

## Map (optional)

While a map is on screen (choosing a place, the Home screen with an active place, or adjusting an alert distance), the map is provided by your phone's own maps service: Google Maps on Android and Apple Maps on iPhone. That provider receives what any map app sends it, such as the area you are viewing, your IP address and technical identifiers of your device, and handles them under its own privacy policy. It does not receive the names of your places or your list of saved places from the app. Nothing is requested while no map is on screen, and the map is never used in the background. If you search for a place or an address in the map picker, the text you type is sent to your phone's search service (Google on Android, Apple on iPhone) only when you submit the search, and the service returns matching places. We do not store the text or the results; only the place you finally choose is saved, on your device. Searching is optional: you can always place the pin by hand.

## Location

The app uses your location for three things, and for nothing else:

1. To detect when you enter or leave the reminder zones you created. This is done by your phone's operating system, which wakes the app when you cross a zone.
2. Each time the system reports a crossing, the app asks your phone for one position fix taken at that moment, to confirm the crossing was real and not a signal error. The fix is used for that decision and is not stored.
3. While the app is open, to show your position on the map, to place a pin where you are, and to order places by distance. This stops when you close the map or the app.

Your position history is never recorded or transmitted. The location is provided by your phone's location services (on Android, Google Play services), which handle it under their own terms.

## Notifications

An alert shows the name you gave to the place. Whether that text is visible on a locked screen, in the notification history or on a connected watch is controlled by your phone's notification and lock-screen settings.

## Purchases

Stop Ahead Pro is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never receive your payment details. The app only asks the store whether Pro is owned and keeps the answer on your device so Pro keeps working offline.

## Diagnostics sharing (optional)

In Settings > Help and support you can choose to create a diagnostic report to send to support: a technical file containing your app version, OS version, device model, recent system events, and reminder outcomes, with no location coordinates and no place names. The app only saves the file where you choose; it is never uploaded by the app and it is shared only if you send it yourself.

## Data retention

Reminder history and the diagnostic log are kept locally and automatically deleted after 30 days. Places and settings stay until you delete them. You can delete all your data at any time from Settings → Delete all my data.

## Who is responsible, and your rights

The app is published by **GuMeLabs**, an independent developer (the name shown in the app stores). Because your places, history and settings exist only on your device, we do not receive them and cannot read, change or delete them for you. You exercise your rights directly in the app: you see your data in the app, you export it from Settings (portability), you correct it by editing a place, and you erase it with Delete all my data. The legal basis for using your location is your consent, given through the system permission, which you can withdraw at any time in your phone's settings.

If you believe your data has been handled unlawfully you may complain to your national data protection authority (in Italy, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali).

## Other companies involved

Google (Google Maps, Google Play services, Google Play) and Apple (Apple Maps, the App Store) process the data described in the Map and Purchases sections under their own privacy policies, and may do so outside your country, including in the United States. Stop Ahead does not send them your saved places.

## Children

Stop Ahead is not designed for children and does not knowingly collect their data.
